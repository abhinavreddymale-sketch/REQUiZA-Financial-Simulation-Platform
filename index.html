SEBI says

Fake trading-app scams lure victims on social media, show fabricated profits, pressure them to invest more, then block withdrawals. Verify intermediaries; avoid unregistered platforms and guaranteed-return claims.

These figures describe the wider cyber-fraud environment. They do not mean all cyber fraud is investment fraud.

Quantum-inspired lab

Quantum-inspired (runs on a classical computer). Not a quantum computer. Not evidence of better returns.

Pick 2 of 5 fictional assets. Classical simulated annealing minimises a QUBO-style energy: risk, minus a reward for return, plus a penalty for not choosing exactly 2. The allocation starts as many possible states and settles on one, an educational analogy for multi-state representation. A brute-force check of all 32 combinations shows whether annealing found the best.
